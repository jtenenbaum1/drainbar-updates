DrainBar support

DrainBar shows usage, reset times, and pacing in your Mac's menu bar. It requires macOS 14 or later. Provider accounts and subscriptions are separate from DrainBar; buying DrainBar does not include any AI-service quota.

Start here

Open DrainBar and choose Try Sample Data to explore the interface without connecting a provider. To connect your own usage, sign in using the provider's own tool, turn off Show sample data in Settings → General, and enable the provider in Settings → Accounts.

The Mac App Store edition asks you to choose your .claude or .codex folder. Hidden folders are shown in the picker; press Command–Shift–G and enter ~/.claude or ~/.codex if needed. Access is read-only. Local logs can contain conversation content; DrainBar extracts usage and timing without saving or sending that content.

Troubleshooting

The meters are empty. Check Settings → Accounts. Enable a provider, grant its folder if requested, and sign in using its own tool. Choose Refresh in DrainBar. Try sample data to inspect the interface while connecting.

Sign-in expired. Renew your sign-in in Claude Code or Codex, then refresh DrainBar. DrainBar does not renew or modify the credentials itself.

Keychain access needed. In the direct-download edition, unlock your Mac and choose Refresh. If macOS asks, approve access only for the DrainBar app you trust. A temporarily locked Keychain does not erase a still-valid token already held in memory. Sandboxed access can differ from the direct-download edition.

Local reading or fallback. Codex may show the latest usage event from local session files when live usage is unavailable. Its timestamp shows the age of that reading; it is not a guarantee of current account usage. Provider service changes can affect live integration availability.

No meters enabled. Open Settings → Accounts and enable Claude or Codex.

The pacing mark. The mark is a forecast baseline for the quota window, not a provider-imposed limit. Personalized pacing learns from optional local usage history. Forecasts may differ from the provider's actual accounting.

Privacy controls. Settings → Refresh & Pacing lets you turn personalized pacing off or clear its local history. See the privacy policy for details.

Updates. App Store purchases update through the Mac App Store. Direct downloads update through DrainBar's Check for Updates command.

Get help

Open a support issue. Include your DrainBar version, macOS version, and the visible error message. Issues are public: never attach provider authentication files, tokens, passwords, session logs, or private conversations.

DrainBar is an independent utility and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic or OpenAI. Provider names identify compatible tools.
