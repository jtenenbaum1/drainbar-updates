DrainBar support

DrainBar shows usage, reset times, and pacing in your Mac's menu bar. It requires macOS 14 or later. Provider accounts and subscriptions are separate from DrainBar; buying DrainBar does not include any AI-service quota.

Start here

Open DrainBar and choose Try Sample Data to explore the interface without granting folder access. Turn off Show sample data in Settings → General when you are ready to view your own local usage.

The Mac App Store edition asks you to choose your .codex folder. Hidden folders are shown in the picker; press Command–Shift–G and enter ~/.codex if needed. Access is read-only. This edition reads local quota metadata, does not read your sign-in credentials, and does not connect to OpenAI or Anthropic services.

The direct-download edition also supports Claude Code and optional live provider usage using your existing provider sign-ins.

Troubleshooting

The meter is empty. In the App Store edition, open Settings → Accounts, enable Codex, allow access to the Codex folder, and choose Refresh. Use Codex normally so its local sessions contain recent quota metadata. Try sample data to inspect the interface while setting this up.

Sign-in expired. This message applies to the direct-download edition. Renew your sign-in in Claude Code or Codex, then refresh DrainBar. DrainBar does not renew or modify credentials itself.

Keychain access needed. This message applies to the direct-download edition. Unlock your Mac and choose Refresh. If macOS asks, approve access only for the DrainBar app you trust.

Local reading. Codex can show the latest quota event from local session files. Its timestamp shows the age of that reading; it is not a guarantee of current account usage.

No meter enabled. Open Settings → Accounts and enable Codex. The direct-download edition can also enable Claude.

The pacing mark. The mark is a forecast baseline for the quota window, not a provider-imposed limit. Personalized pacing learns from optional local usage history. Forecasts may differ from the provider's actual accounting.

Privacy controls. Settings → Refresh & Pacing lets you turn personalized pacing off or clear its local history. See the privacy policy for details.

Updates. App Store purchases update through the Mac App Store. Direct downloads update through DrainBar's Check for Updates command.

Get help

Open a support issue. Include your DrainBar version, macOS version, and the visible error message. Issues are public: never attach provider authentication files, tokens, passwords, session logs, or private conversations.

DrainBar is an independent utility and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic or OpenAI. Provider names identify compatible tools.
